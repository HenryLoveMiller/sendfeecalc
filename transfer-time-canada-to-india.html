How long does it take to send money from Canada to India?

The honest answer is that the provider is not what decides. The payout rail on the Indian side does — and the way you fund the transfer decides the fee. The short answer

Minutes to a few hours, or up to two business days — and the difference is the rail, not the brand. ICICI Bank Canada publishes that a transfer is credited instantaneously when the recipient's account is enabled for IMPS, and that if the recipient is not registered for IMPS the transfer goes through NEFT or RTGS, which may take up to 48 business hours. Taptap Send publishes within a few minutes for UPI delivery and under 2 hours for a bank transfer that may still take 2 working days to appear.

Apps publish the fastest numbers. Wise publishes that 77% of transfers arrive in under 20 seconds and 95% in less than a day. Remitly publishes that over 90% of funded transfers are delivered in under an hour, with most in seconds.

How you pay changes both the clock and the price. On Wise's own published table, funding with a debit card arrives in seconds and costs 57.33 CAD on 1,000 CAD; funding by bank transfer costs 2.28 CAD but arrives on a set date instead of instantly. Fastest and cheapest are different rows.

Questions people actually ask

How long does it take to send money from Canada to India?

It depends on two separate things: how you fund the transfer, and how the money reaches the recipient. Wise publishes arrival times by funding method — a Wise account, direct debit, debit card, credit card, Google Pay and Apple Pay are shown as arriving in seconds, INTERAC e-Transfer in 5 minutes, and a bank transfer on a specific date. Remitly publishes that over 90% of funded transfers are delivered in under an hour, with most in seconds, and that some eligible transfers may arrive within minutes while others can take a few business days. ICICI Bank Canada publishes that a transfer is credited instantaneously when the recipient's account is enabled for IMPS, and that NEFT/RTGS transfers may take up to 48 business hours. Taptap Send publishes within a few minutes for UPI, and under 2 hours for a bank transfer, which may still take 2 working days to appear on the recipient's account.

Which is faster, a money transfer app or a bank wire?

On the figures these providers publish, an app is usually faster. Wise publishes that 77% of its transfers arrive in under 20 seconds and 95% in less than a day. Remitly publishes that over 90% of funded transfers are delivered in under an hour, most in seconds. ICICI Bank Canada publishes up to 48 business hours for NEFT/RTGS transfers, and instant credit only for IMPS-enabled accounts. The deciding factor is usually the payout rail on the Indian side rather than the sender's brand.

What is IMPS, NEFT, RTGS and UPI, and which one is fastest?

IMPS, NEFT and RTGS are India's bank-to-bank payment systems; UPI is the instant-payment system that most people in India use day to day. ICICI Bank Canada publishes that a transfer is credited instantaneously if the recipient's account is enabled for IMPS, and that if the recipient is not registered for IMPS the transfer goes via NEFT or RTGS, which may take up to 48 business hours. Taptap Send publishes within a few minutes for UPI delivery. So the rail the recipient can receive on decides the wait far more than the provider you send with.

Does the way I pay change how long it takes?

Yes, and it changes the fee too. Wise publishes a separate arrival time for each funding method, and a separate fee for each on 1,000 CAD: Wise account in seconds at 6.57 CAD, bank transfer on a specific date at 2.28 CAD, direct debit in seconds at 4.33 CAD, debit card in seconds at 57.33 CAD, credit card in seconds at 33.51 CAD, INTERAC e-Transfer in 5 minutes at 13.47 CAD, and Google Pay or Apple Pay in seconds at 33.51 CAD. Funding by card is the fastest on Wise's own table and also the most expensive.

Is a faster transfer worth paying more for?

That is a price question, not a speed question, and it depends on how urgent the money is. Use the delivery times on this page to find out which routes can be fast for your recipient's rail, then use the calculator to see what each channel actually delivers after fees and spread. If the money is not urgent, the cheapest funding method on the same provider will usually land on the same rail anyway, a day or two later.
