Known limits of this page

  • Provider fees depend on the amount sent and the payment method, not only on the corridor. The fee shown applies to the amount on the page unless the row says otherwise.
  • Welcome, bonus and promotional rates are one-time offers with an amount cap and eligibility conditions. The everyday rate is the number to plan on.
  • Several providers publish no figure for a repeat transfer. We print no number there rather than one that looks like a fact.
  • Delivery times are compared on a separate page, how long it takes to send money from Canada to India, using the figures read on 10 November 2026. No two providers publish the same kind of figure — one a percentage of its own transfers, one an average by funding method, one a rule about payment rails, one a per-method table — so they are printed as published and never blended into a single average. A provider's own estimate for a specific transfer, shown before you confirm, is the authoritative one.
  • No referral links are live on this site yet, so no provider pays us. If that changes, it will be marked at the point the link appears, before you click.

Corrections

If a figure here is out of date or wrong, it is our error. Check the provider's own page first, and treat the provider's page as the authority — this site is a reading aid, not a source of truth.
