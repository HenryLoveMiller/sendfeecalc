The short answer

On the published figure, ICICI Bank Canada's Money2India rate beat the mid-market by 765 INR on 1,000 CAD, with no transfer fee on a first transfer. The published rate was 68.5616 INR per CAD against a mid-market reference of 67.797 for the same business date.

But that figure is a bonus rate for a first transfer, and the second number matters more. ICICI publishes it with the disclaimer that it is a bonus FX rate for a new customer on their first transfer up to C$1,000, subject to eligibility and payment method. We could not find a published rate for a repeat transfer, so we do not print one — and that is the number to plan on.

What the published wording actually says

The terms matter more than the headline rate, because the rate is only available to some transfers. These are the published statements, in their own words.

"FX Rate on Money2India C$ 1 = ₹ 68.5616. Disclaimer: Bonus FX Rate for New Customer on their 1st transfer upto C$1000. Subject to eligibility and payment method. T&C apply."icicibank.ca homepage, read 4 November 2026
"Our FX Rate for You $ 1 = ₹ 68.5616. FX rate will be applied on all money transfers made during the offer period. *Disclaimer: Bonus FX Rate for New Customer on their 1st transfer upto C$1000. Subject to eligibility and payment method. T&C apply."icicibank.ca — FX Rate page, read 4 November 2026
"New Customer Offer for Money2India (Canada) — Enjoy ₹2 bonus rate and zero fees on your first transfer up to $1,000 — new customers only. *T&Cs Apply"icicibank.ca — Money2India (Canada) offer terms, read 4 November 2026

Five things follow from that text, and this page is built around them.

  • It is a bonus rate, not a base rate. The published figure is explicitly a bonus FX rate for a new customer on a first transfer, up to C$1,000. The second transfer is priced differently, and the pages do not publish what that is — so this page does not print a repeat-transfer number.
  • Zero fees belong to the same first transfer. They are part of the new-customer offer, up to $1,000, not a standing feature of the service.
  • Both ICICI pages carried the same number when we read them. The homepage and the FX rate page both showed 68.5616 on the same read, so the figure above is one rate published in two places, not two competing rates.
  • Eligibility and payment method are conditions, not footnotes. ICICI states the bonus is subject to both, which means the published rate is a ceiling rather than a guarantee for every transfer.
  • You do not need to switch banks. ICICI Bank states that Money2India Canada is open to both ICICI Bank Canada and non-ICICI Bank Canada account holders, and transfers can be made from any Canadian bank account to any bank in India.

Does ICICI Money2India charge a transfer fee from Canada?

On a first transfer up to C$1,000, the published offer is a bonus rate with zero fees, for new customers only. We could not read a published transfer fee for a second transfer or for amounts above C$1,000, so we do not print one.

Is the published Money2India rate available on every transfer?

No. The rate ICICI publishes carries the disclaimer that it is a bonus FX rate for a new customer on their first transfer up to C$1,000, subject to eligibility and payment method, with terms and conditions applying. Both the homepage and the FX rate page carried the same figure when we read them, but neither publishes a rate for a repeat transfer, so we do not print one.

Do I need an ICICI Bank Canada account to use Money2India?

No. ICICI Bank states that Money2India Canada is open to both ICICI Bank Canada and non-ICICI Bank Canada account holders, and transfers can be made from any Canadian bank account to any bank in India.
