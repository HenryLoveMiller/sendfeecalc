Privacy, funding and contact

Free to use. No sign-up, no advertising, no tracking. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

The short version. This site is free and does not ask for anything. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no sign-up form. You can read every page without telling us who you are.

What this site is

Send Fee Calculator is a free reference page. It shows what money transfer providers publish about what a recipient actually receives, after the transfer fee and the exchange rate spread are taken out. Every figure was read from that provider's own published page, on the date shown in the table. Nothing on this site is estimated.

How this site is funded

  • There are no advertisements on this site.
  • You are never asked to pay, and there is nothing to buy.
  • If we ever add referral links to a provider, we will label every one of them as a referral link on the page where it appears, and this page will say so. We will never let a referral link change a price, a rate, or which provider is listed.
  • We are not a money transfer service. We do not hold, send, or receive money, and we cannot move your funds. Every rate here is a published number copied from the provider, not a quote we can offer you.
  • This is not financial advice. It is a summary of published prices. Confirm the final amount on the provider's own site before you send.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, and no advertising tags are loaded. The page does not load any third-party script.
  • There is no account, no sign-up form, and no newsletter.
  • The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The amount you type is not sent to us and is not stored anywhere.

One exception, and it is not tracking: the calculator reads the current mid-market reference rate from the European Central Bank's public reference feed (via api.frankfurter.dev) so the gap column has a live benchmark. That request sends no information about you and sets no identifier.

What the hosting provider sees

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the technical information needed to deliver the page — your IP address, browser type, and the requested URL — for security and abuse prevention. That information is covered by GitHub's own privacy terms, not by anything on this page. We do not have access to per-visitor logs, and we do not use them.

Third-party links

Pages here link to providers such as Wise, Remitly, Taptap Send, and ICICI Bank, and to the European Central Bank. Those are outbound links to other companies. Once you follow one, you are on their site and their own privacy policy and terms apply to what happens there. We have no control over them and no access to anything you do after you leave.

How to reach a human

Corrections, disputes about a number, or a provider you want compared — open an issue on the site's public repository:

github.com/HenryLoveMiller/sendfeecalc/issues

If a figure on this site is wrong, tell us which page and what the provider actually publishes. We will correct it and update the read date on the sources page — we do not quietly change a number and leave the old date on it.
