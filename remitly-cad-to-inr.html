Remitly CAD to INR: which published rate actually applies to you

Remitly publishes two CAD to INR rates for Canada senders, and they are 1.60 rupees apart. The one in the advert is not the one on your second transfer. Both are priced below, on the provider's own published figures — and so is the fee line that only applies below C$500. How this is built. Every rate and fee below was read from Remitly's own published pages on 3 November 2026 and is quoted as published — not estimated, not averaged. The mid-market reference is the European Central Bank rate for the same business date (67.797). Rates move during the day; the rate in the app before you confirm is the one that applies to your transfer.

The short answer

There are two published rates, and the gap between them is worth about 1,600 INR on every 1,000 CAD. The welcome rate we read was 68.11 INR per CAD — new customers, first transfer only, capped at the first C$2,000, one per customer. The everyday rate we read was 66.51 INR per CAD, and that is the rate from your second transfer onwards.

The fee line is smaller than most write-ups make it sound. Remitly's published fee for this corridor is C$1.99 below C$500 sent and C$0 at C$500 or above. On a 1,000 CAD transfer the fee is zero either way — the whole cost sits inside the rate.

Against mid-market, the two rates land on opposite sides. The welcome rate beat the 3 November reference by 313 INR on 1,000 CAD. The everyday rate sat 1,287 INR behind it. A zero transfer fee did not make the everyday rate cheap; the margin is in the 66.51.

Questions people actually ask

What is Remitly's CAD to INR exchange rate?

Remitly publishes two different CAD to INR rates for Canada senders. The welcome rate we read on 3 November 2026 was 68.11 INR per CAD, for new customers on their first transfer, capped at the first C$2,000. The everyday rate we read the same day was 66.51 INR per CAD, and it is the rate that applies from your second transfer onwards. The rate shown in the app when you start a transfer is the one that applies to you, and it changes during the day.

Does Remitly charge a fee to send from Canada to India?

Remitly's published fee line for this corridor is C$1.99 when you send less than C$500, and C$0 at C$500 or above. The welcome-rate transfer carries no transfer fee. Remitly builds most of its cost into the exchange rate rather than the fee line, which is why a zero-fee quote is not the same thing as a cheap transfer.

How much does 1,000 CAD send to India through Remitly?

At the welcome rate of 68.11 we read on 3 November 2026, 1,000 CAD delivers about 68,110 INR, which is 313 INR ahead of that day's mid-market reference of 67.797. At the everyday rate of 66.51 it delivers about 66,510 INR, which is 1,287 INR behind the same reference. The difference between the two rates on the same 1,000 CAD is about 1,600 INR.

Can I get Remitly's welcome rate on my second transfer?

No. Remitly's own terms make the welcome rate a one-time offer for new customers, one per customer, applying to the first C$2,000 of the first transfer. From the second transfer onwards the everyday rate applies. If you send money to India regularly, price the everyday rate, not the advertised one.

Why is the Remitly rate I see different from the advertised rate?

The advertised number is the welcome rate, which only applies to a new customer's first transfer up to C$2,000. The rate shown in the app is your live everyday rate, which changes during the day and sits lower — we read 66.51 on 3 November 2026 against an advertised 68.11. Neither number is wrong; they answer different questions. The rate applied to your transfer is the one in the app before you confirm.
